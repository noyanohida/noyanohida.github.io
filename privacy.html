Privacy Policy — Upload New CS2 Videos

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Upload New CS2 Videos" (the "App"), a personal desktop tool operated by NOYANO HIDA, handles data when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Data we access

With your permission via TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, and uses the video.publish scope to publish videos you choose to your TikTok account.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to authenticate you and to upload and publish your own CS2 stream clips to your account. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the creator's own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties other than TikTok itself. Video files remain on the creator's computer until uploaded to TikTok.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Data is transmitted only to TikTok's official API to perform the upload.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is authorized. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Manage app permissions); local tokens are then deleted. You may also request deletion by email.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: noyanohida.business@gmail.com
